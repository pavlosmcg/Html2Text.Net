NAND2Tetris Special Guest Talk - Florindo Santoro

We are delighted to welcome Florindo Santoro who will be giving a talk on how to bring the Hack platform to life using FPGAs.
Florindo is an electronics engineer specialized in FPGA and Digital Design working for fabless company Accent building ASIC SystemOnChips in Italy. Florindo is in the UK for a limited time only and will be returning to Italy a few daysafter his talk, so we are particularly grateful for his generosity with his time during a holiday!
Schedule:
18:30 - Arrival, networking/chat, food if you need to eat.
19:00 - Talk begins followed by practical demo.
20:00 - Discussion and retrospective on Course so far. Proposal for the TECSWeb project
21:00 - Close. Optional further networking/chat, etc.
Preparation
No preparation is required this week, although having read through chapters 1-6 of the course book will help place the talk in context.
How to find us:
Follow signs to Park Street Car Park; the Maypole is next door. We will be in the upstairs room.
